About Shane

Ten years in the trade.

Small-town Kansas roots, a decade of hands-on experience, and a crew built on the idea that most painting problems start before the first coat goes on.

Shane grew up in a small town in Kansas — the kind of place where if something needs fixing, you figure it out yourself. He got into the painting trade the same way he gets into most things: started at the bottom, paid attention, and didn't cut corners on the work even when it would've been easier to.

Before going out on his own, Shane spent years working with JT Paint Design, where he learned what separates a paint job that holds up from one that just looks good at the walk-through. Prep work. Material selection. Getting the surface right before you ever open a can. Those habits stuck.

Going independent wasn't about undercutting anyone. It was about being able to control the standard on every job — not just the ones where there was time to do it right.

SK Painting Solutions has been built almost entirely on repeat business and referrals. Shane doesn't run ads. He doesn't bid low to win the job and hope no one notices the cut corners later. His pricing reflects what the work actually costs to do well — premium materials, real prep time, and a crew that knows what they're doing.

The business is growing — carefully. Shane's second crew member has been with him for over a year and a half, and they're actively adding to the team. The goal isn't to be the biggest painting company in Tulsa. It's to be the one people keep calling back.

On materials: SK uses premium paints — not because it's a selling point to put on a website, but because cheap paint fails faster and Shane would rather not get a call two years later about a job he thought was done. The materials cost more upfront. The work lasts longer.

On pricing: He's not the cheapest painter you can find in Bartlesville or Tulsa. He knows that, and he'll tell you that when you ask. What you're paying for is someone who shows up when he said he would, does the prep work properly, and doesn't disappear after the job. Most of his clients are on their second or third project with him.

There's nothing we won't paint. Rental turnovers, custom cabinetry, full exterior repaints, drywall repair before the first coat — all of it.

The construction repair work came out of a practical reality: a lot of surfaces need attention before they can be painted properly. Drywall that's been patched badly, trim that needs to be reset, minor framing work that's holding up the whole project. Shane and his crew can handle all of it, which means a client rarely has to coordinate multiple contractors to get a room done.
