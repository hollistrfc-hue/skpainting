What we do

There's not much we won't paint.

Interior rooms, full exterior repaints, rental turnovers, trim and cabinetry, drywall repair — SK handles the whole range. If the job involves paint and something that needs to be right before it goes on, we can take it.

Interior Painting

Residential  ·  Year-round

Interior painting is the bulk of what SK does, and it's where the detail orientation shows most. Shane works through rooms the way most people wish their painter would — proper prep, the right primer for the surface, clean cut lines, and a finish that doesn't need to be redone next season.

Single rooms, full homes, new construction interiors, occupied homes — all of it. The work is done with premium paint that's specified for the surface type, not whatever's on sale. That choice matters more than most clients realize when they're looking at the walls two years later.

Exterior Painting

Residential  ·  Seasonal (spring–fall)

Exterior painting is weather-dependent work, and Shane treats it that way. That means scheduling around conditions that actually matter — temperature, humidity, drying time — not just getting the job done and moving on. The prep work on an exterior is where most painters cut corners and where failures always start.

Wood, fiber cement, stucco, brick, metal trim — different surfaces need different prep and different products. SK uses exterior paints rated for longevity, not just appearance on day one. A good exterior paint job should last years, not one Oklahoma summer.

Rental Property Repaints

Landlord & property owner work

Turnover repaints are a different kind of job. The priority is efficiency and durability — getting a unit move-in ready without dragging out the process. Shane works with landlords and property owners who need a reliable painter for between-tenant work, not someone who disappears for two weeks on a one-bedroom unit.

Rental properties need paint that holds up under real occupancy conditions — marks, cleaning, furniture contact. SK specs materials for durability, not just appearance. The finish looks good on move-in day and keeps looking reasonable through the lease.

Detail & Fine Finish Work

Trim  ·  Cabinetry  ·  Precision work

This is where most painters struggle and where Shane's experience shows. Trim, crown molding, built-ins, kitchen cabinets, custom millwork — the kind of work where you can see every brush stroke if it's not done right. It requires preparation, the right applicator for the surface, and the patience to do multiple thin coats instead of one heavy one.

Cabinet repaints in particular have become a regular part of the business. Sprayed or brushed depending on the piece, prepped and primed correctly, finished in a product that won't yellow or chip with normal kitchen use. It's a fraction of the cost of new cabinets and holds up longer than most people expect.

Construction Repair

Drywall  ·  Carpentry  ·  Prep work  ·  Year-round

Most paint jobs have something that needs to be fixed before the first coat goes on. Drywall that got damaged during a renovation, a doorframe that's been patched badly, texture that needs to match the rest of the ceiling. SK handles that repair work in-house, which means a client doesn't have to coordinate two separate contractors to get from start to finish.

This side of the business also keeps the crew working through winter. Drywall repair, texture matching, minor framing and carpentry, prep work that gets pushed to the off-season — Shane's schedule has room for it when exterior work slows down, and the quality is consistent with the rest of the work he does.
