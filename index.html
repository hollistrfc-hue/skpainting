Available year-round

Interior work doesn't wait for warm weather.

Exterior painting slows down in winter — that's just how it goes in Oklahoma. But interior painting, trim work, drywall repair, and prep work run year-round, and Shane's schedule reflects that. If you've been putting off a project, January is actually a better window than August.

The construction repair side of the business was built specifically to fill that gap — so the crew stays working and clients don't have to wait until spring to get things done.

Good winter projects
  • Interior painting — any room, any scope
  • Trim and door painting
  • Cabinet repaints
  • Drywall repair and patching
  • Texture work before paint
  • Minor carpentry prep
