Where we work

Northeast Oklahoma.

SK Painting Solutions is based in Bartlesville and works regularly in Owasso and across the greater Tulsa metro. If you're not sure whether we cover your neighborhood, reach out — the answer is usually yes.

The work that comes in is mostly residential — homeowners, landlords managing rentals, and the occasional light commercial project. Shane knows the Bartlesville market the way you'd expect from someone who's built the business almost entirely on referrals from it. Owasso and the Tulsa area have followed from there.

Bartlesville Washington County, OK

Bartlesville is home base. Most of SK's work started here and spread outward as referrals accumulated. The neighborhoods in Bartlesville tend to have older homes with real character — original trim, plaster walls in some of the older sections, wood siding that needs to be handled properly rather than just rolled over. Shane's been through enough of them to know what to expect and how to prep for it.

Interior and exterior painting, rental property turnovers, cabinet repaints, construction repair — the full scope is available here. Work is available year-round, and Shane's schedule generally has more room in winter months than it does heading into spring and summer.

Owasso Tulsa County, OK

Owasso is a regular part of SK's work schedule. The residential market is active — a lot of newer builds in the area alongside established neighborhoods — and both interior and exterior projects come in regularly. Shane works here on the same standard as everywhere else: right materials, real prep, no shortcuts to speed up the timeline.

Owasso clients tend to find SK through referrals from neighbors or from Bartlesville connections. If you've gotten a recommendation to call, that's the most common path in. The process is the same regardless: a look at the scope, a straight conversation about what it'll take, and a quote that reflects the actual work.

Greater Tulsa Tulsa & surrounding counties, OK

The Tulsa metro and surrounding communities are within SK's regular working range. Collinsville, Claremore, Broken Arrow, Sand Springs — if it's within a reasonable drive from Bartlesville, the answer to "do you cover my area" is almost always yes. The scope and pricing don't change based on location.

For larger projects in the Tulsa metro — full exterior repaints, interior painting across multiple rooms, or properties with significant construction repair needs — Shane will do an in-person walk-through before quoting. That's standard practice regardless of where the job is. It's the only way to give a number that's actually accurate.
